# My Academic Journey

Welcome to my first blog! My name is **Ahmad Talib**, and I am a Computer Engineering student from **Faisalabad, Pakistan**. This blog is a reflection of my academic journey from the day I entered university until the completion of my second semester. Throughout these semesters, I have gained valuable knowledge, worked on meaningful projects, and developed both technical and personal skills. In this post, I would like to share how my learning journey has evolved over time.

---

# 1. Admission Journey

### 1.1 Starting My University Life

Getting admission to the Computer Engineering program was an exciting milestone in my life. I was eager to begin learning about programming, software development, and modern technologies. Although everything seemed new and challenging, I was determined to make the most of this opportunity and build a strong foundation for my future career.

### 1.2 Adapting to a New Learning Environment

The first few weeks at university required me to adjust to a completely different learning environment. I attended lectures, participated in laboratory sessions, and collaborated with classmates on assignments. This experience taught me the importance of discipline, time management, and teamwork.

### 1.3 Setting My Academic Goals

From the beginning, I wanted to become more than just a student who completed assignments. My goal was to understand concepts deeply, develop practical skills, build real-world projects, and continuously improve myself. This mindset motivated me throughout every semester.

---

# 2. First Semester

### 2.1 Learning Database Fundamentals

During my first semester, one of the most interesting subjects was Database Systems. I learned about relational databases, ER diagrams, normalization, primary keys, foreign keys, and SQL queries. These concepts helped me understand how data is organized and managed efficiently in software applications.

### 2.2 Working on the DM Mart Prediction Project

Our major semester project was **DM Mart Prediction**, which focused on retail business data. Through this project, I learned how customer information, product details, and sales records could be stored in a structured database. It was my first opportunity to apply theoretical database concepts in a practical project.

### 2.3 Lessons Learned from the First Semester

The first semester helped me build confidence in designing databases, writing SQL queries, and working with relational data. It also improved my teamwork, documentation, and presentation skills. Although I was still a beginner, this semester laid the foundation for everything I learned later.

---

# 3. Second Semester

### 3.1 Developing the Diabetes Risk Prediction Project

In the second semester, our project shifted from business data to healthcare. We developed a **Diabetes Risk Prediction** system using a medical dataset. This project showed me that databases are not limited to business applications but also play an essential role in healthcare and intelligent systems.

### 3.2 Understanding the Connection Between Databases and Machine Learning

Our instructor, **Dr. Bilal Ahmad**, explained how high-quality datasets are essential for Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. During this project, I learned that missing values, duplicate records, and poor database design can directly affect prediction accuracy. This experience helped me appreciate the importance of data integrity and database normalization.

### 3.3 Looking Back and Moving Forward

Comparing my first and second semesters, I can clearly see how much I have grown as a Computer Engineering student. From learning basic SQL queries to understanding the relationship between databases and machine learning, every project has contributed to my development. I now look forward to learning more about databases, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and software development while building projects that solve real-world problems.

---

## Conclusion

My journey from admission to completing the second semester has been full of learning, challenges, and personal growth. Every course, assignment, and project has helped me become a more confident learner and a better problem solver. I believe this is only the beginning of my journey, and I am excited to continue exploring new technologies, improving my skills, and sharing my experiences through this blog.
